About Roadmate Car Rental

Providing seamless, reliable, and transparent mobility solutions for visitors and locals across Cebu.

Our Story

Founded with a simple promise — "Your Ride, Ready When You Are" — Roadmate Car Rental started as a small local fleet of 3 vehicles in Mandaue City. Over the years, we have expanded to 3 strategic branches across Metro Cebu, driven by a commitment to upfront pricing, immaculate vehicle upkeep, and warm Filipino hospitality.

Whether you're a tourist embarking on an island-wide road trip or a business professional arriving at Mactan Airport, we handle the logistics so you can focus on the journey.

Mission & Core Values

Integrity First

No hidden fees, no fine print surprises. We believe in clear, transparent pricing and total honesty in every rental agreement.

Uncompromising Safety

Every car undergoes a rigorous 40-point safety check before key handover. Your family's security is our highest priority.

Customer Centricity

From 24/7 roadside assistance to flexible pickup schedules, we adapt our services around your itinerary.

Our Milestone Timeline
