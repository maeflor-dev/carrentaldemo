Security Deposits & Payments

A refundable security deposit ranging from PHP 3,000 to PHP 15,000 (depending on vehicle class) is collected upon vehicle pickup. Deposits are fully refunded within 24 hours after vehicle return provided the car is returned undamaged and with the same fuel level.

License & Age Rules

Drivers must be between 21 and 65 years old and hold a valid driver's license (Philippine LTO license or International Driving Permit) held for at least 12 months. Additional drivers can be registered at no extra cost.

Insurance Coverage

All rentals include basic comprehensive insurance with standard third-party liability. Deductibles apply in the event of at-fault incidents. Optional collision damage waiver upgrades are available during booking.

Fuel & Mileage Policy

Vehicles are handed over with a full tank of fuel and should be returned full (Full-to-Full policy). Unlimited mileage applies for travel within Cebu Province; out-of-province trips require prior notification.
