Top 5 Scenic Road Trips Across Cebu Province

Cebu is more than just bustling urban centers—it is an island of dramatic coastal highways, winding mountain passes, and stunning viewpoints waiting to be explored behind the wheel of a reliable rental car.

1. The South Coastal Highway (Cebu City to Moalboal)

Enjoy sweeping views of the Tañon Strait as you drive south. Stop by Barili for local delicacies or continue down to Moalboal for world-class snorkeling and pristine beaches.

2. The Transcentral Highway (Cebu City to Balamban)

A favorite among local drivers and motorcycle enthusiasts. Experience cool mountain air, panoramic valley lookouts, and popular hillside flower gardens.

3. Northern Coastal Loop (Daanbantayan)

Drive up north to the jump-off point for Malapascua Island. The smooth coastal stretches make for an effortless, relaxing weekend cruise.
